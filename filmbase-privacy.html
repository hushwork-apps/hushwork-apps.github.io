FilmBase Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

FilmBase is a personal film and TV rating app published by Hushwork. This policy describes what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: FilmBase has no accounts, runs no servers of its own, and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.

Information stored on your device

Your ratings, library, watch history, and app preferences are stored in a local database on your device. This data is not transmitted to Hushwork or to any third party by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Film and TV metadata (TMDB)

To show film and show details, posters, and related metadata, the app sends requests to The Movie Database (TMDB) API. These requests include your search terms and the standard technical information any internet request carries, such as your IP address. They do not include your ratings, your library, or any account identifier. TMDB's handling of requests is described in the TMDB privacy policy. Trailer links open in the YouTube app or your browser, outside FilmBase. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Optional Google Drive backup

You can optionally back up your library to your own Google Drive by signing in with your Google account. If you do:

The backup is stored in a hidden application data folder in your Google Drive that only FilmBase can access. The app requests the narrowest Drive permission Google offers (drive.appdata): it cannot see, read, or modify any of your other Drive files. Hushwork has no access to your backup, your Google account, or your Drive. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google account's security settings, and you can delete the backup data from Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

What the app does not do

FilmBase contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or any personal information. It has no user accounts and no server operated by Hushwork.

Children

FilmBase is not directed at children under 13. Film and TV metadata is supplied by TMDB and may include titles intended for mature audiences.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
